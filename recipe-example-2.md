# Old Recipe
Ammamma's Festive Chintapandu Pulihora Notes:
Cook 2 big tumblers of old Sona Masoori raw rice. The grains MUST stay completely separate—spread the hot rice on a wide brass thali, drizzle 2 tablespoons of raw groundnut oil and a teaspoon of turmeric over it while still steaming, and don't touch it until cool.

Pulihora paste (Pulusu): Soak a large fistful of dry dark tamarind in hot water and extract thick pulp. Boil the pulp on medium flame with 4 slit green chillies, 1 teaspoon turmeric, rock salt, a piece of jaggery the size of a coin, and a roasted methi-mustard seed powder (half spoon). Simmer until the oil starts leaving the sides.

Tempering: Heat 3 tbsp groundnut oil in a heavy kadai. Add 1 tbsp mustard seeds (let them crackle completely), 2 tbsp chana dal, 1 tbsp urad dal, half cup raw peanuts (fry till deep golden and crunchy), 4 broken dried red chillies, a generous quarter spoon of asafoetida (hing), and lots of fresh curry leaves.

Mix the cooled tamarind paste into the rice with hands gently so grains don't break, then pour the crunchy tadka on top. 
Note: "Must have a sharp tangy kick, and the peanuts must stay crunchy till evening."

# Cravings
Keep the sour-spicy tang and crunchy peanut bite, but replace the refined white rice and avoid the jaggery for blood sugar control without making the grain mushy.
